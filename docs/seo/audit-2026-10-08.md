## Article recommendations

Proposed titles below are HTML search titles, not mandatory replacements for the editorial H1. Search intent is a hypothesis to validate with Search Console. Proposed summaries for the seven new pieces are already implemented; proposed summaries for the original four are optional copy changes.

### New Orleans: second lines

- Proposed title: **New Orleans Second Lines: Community, Music, and Mutual Aid | Folkly**
- Proposed description: “Explore how New Orleans second lines connect brass-band music, social aid and pleasure clubs, and traditions of community care.”
- Explain “second line” early and distinguish the parade followers from the band/first line. Keep social aid and pleasure clubs central; avoid reducing the practice to a visitor attraction.
- Add links to Detroit techno for community-built music and Tarragona castells for public participation. Retain the existing listening examples; identify the performer and recording context.
- Keep the parade photograph's specific attribution and date. Check the schema author against the visible Folkly editorial byline.

### Lisbon: fado

- Proposed title: **Lisbon Fado: Neighborhoods, History, and Belonging | Folkly**
- Proposed description: “Discover how fado grew from Lisbon's neighborhoods into a musical tradition of memory and belonging, with recordings to hear its sound.”
- Introduce fado and Alfama by name near the beginning. Separate Lisbon's tradition from Coimbra fado if discussing both; avoid implying one neighborhood represents the whole history.
- Link to Detroit techno and New Orleans second lines. Keep the Museu do Fado listening examples with named singers and the musical point each example illustrates.
- The photograph is an Alfama street, not a pictured performance. Retain that precise caption. Produce smaller responsive image variants.

### Oaxaca: Zapotec weaving

- Proposed title: **Zapotec Weaving in Teotitlán del Valle, Oaxaca | Folkly**
- Proposed description: “Explore Zapotec weaving in Teotitlán del Valle, where natural dyes, family knowledge, and changing designs connect craft to place.”
- Name Teotitlán del Valle and Zapotec weaving early; distinguish this community from Oaxaca's many other textile traditions. Explain the materials actually documented in the sources.
- Link to Bonwire kente and Tokushima indigo through the shared themes of knowledge transmission and textile livelihoods.
- Prioritize responsive variants of the 4.4 MiB portrait image. Keep the editorial language without turning color meanings into universal claims.

- Add a licensed, historically relevant photograph or artwork with an accurate context caption. The current grid deliberately uses a text card; it does not invent an image. A genuine image would also enable Article image and social image metadata.

### Bonwire: kente

## Verification and limits

Focused checks cover all 11 grid entries, a future published entry appearing automatically, a draft canary never appearing, unsafe/duplicate routes failing the build, credited image metadata, clean article canonicals, sitemap inclusion, existing local image availability, and unchanged article bodies/source lists. Existing public-output and security suites remain required.

Those hashes document the reviewed downloads; external image URLs are not enforced by a runtime integrity mechanism. Lead images use the same files for actual content and preview metadata, not fabricated image URLs. Legacy photos keep their existing attribution.

Live deployment checks, Search Console indexing, Rich Results validation, mobile rendering, and Core Web Vitals are separate evidence. No search-performance improvement is claimed before measurement. Automated production, publication, and the recurring article schedule remain disabled.

## Publication rule

For a future release: first satisfy review/authorization and retain the verified source/evidence; export its public HTML route; then add its public record to `web/vercel/articles.json` with `status: published`, publication date, title, complete summary, place, `placeName`, `placeSlug`, topic slugs, and verified image metadata when available. Add `searchTitle`, a verified `authorSlug` for persona work, and contextual `related` references as appropriate. Active place/topic routes, archive counts, persona lists, and related links are generated from this record. Commit both together and run the build/tests. The catalog populates cards and sitemap, but does not publish reserve content or enable the publisher. The eventual publisher must update this public export atomically after its acceptance gates pass.
